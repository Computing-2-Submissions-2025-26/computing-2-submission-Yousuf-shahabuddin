How to play

The goal

Score the most points by decorating the wall of your player board with coloured tiles. The game ends after any round in which a player has completed a full horizontal row of five tiles on their wall.

Each round (Factory Offer)

  1. On your turn, pick all tiles of one colour from one factory display or from the centre of the table.
  2. If you picked from a factory, the leftover tiles in that factory slide into the centre.
  3. The first player to pick from the centre each round also takes the 1 first-player token. The token goes on their floor line (costing a point) and decides who starts next round.
  4. Place all the tiles you picked onto one of your five pattern lines. Line 1 holds 1 tile; line 5 holds 5.
  5. Tiles in a pattern line must all be the same colour, and the colour cannot already be in the corresponding row of your wall.
  6. Any tiles you can't place (or pick to discard) fall on your floor line.

End of round (Wall-tiling)

Each complete pattern line moves one tile onto the matching colour space in its wall row. You score immediately:

  • If the new tile has no neighbours, score 1.
  • Otherwise count every tile connected to it horizontally and vertically (including the new tile itself). Add both runs to your score.

Then subtract floor-line penalties (-1, -1, -2, -2, -2, -3, -3 for the seven floor positions). Your score cannot drop below zero.

End of game (Bonus)

  • +2 per complete horizontal row
  • +7 per complete vertical column
  • +10 per colour with all 5 placed

Highest score wins. Ties broken by more completed rows.

Controls

Typically played with a mouse. If playing with a keyboard only, use Tab and Shift+Tab to navigate between interactive elements and Enter or Space to activate them. The game is fully playable with keyboard alone, and all actions have accessible labels and instructions.
